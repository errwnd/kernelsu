Flash order for Lunaris ROM on RMX2170 (SM7125)
================================================

1. Lunaris ROM zip  (flash first, clean install)

2. Lightning-McQueen-ksu-0723-20260602-v1.0.1.zip
   (KernelSU kernel — required for root + fp fix)

3. lunaris-rmx2170-bootfix.zip
   (Keymaster HAL interface fix + disable RealmeParts/KeyHandler
    to prevent lockscreen bootloop on Lunaris)

4. rmx2170-udfps-fix-recovery-v1.2.zip
   (UDFPS fingerprint fix: sjdude_fp_engine, init RC, HAL shim wrap,
    sysfs perms — finger works ~10s after boot unlock)

5. Reboot to system

Notes:
- rmx2170-udfps-fix-recovery-v1.1.zip is superseded by v1.2
- Re-flash zips 3 & 4 after any ROM update (they are idempotent)
- Do NOT flash Magisk udfps module on top — it conflicts with zip fix
